**2026 update:** I completed my B.S. at Yonsei University in
February and received the President's Award from the Korean
Society of Economic and Environmental Geology. Since March,
I have been collaborating with the Yonsei University Museum
on Quaternary mammal fossils and working on a national fossil
heritage research project. In April, I presented my research
on a Miocene talpid from Korea.
