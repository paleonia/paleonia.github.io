research

Mammalian paleontology, paleoecology, and the Korean fossil record

My research interests center on how mammalian communities have changed through time and how their fossil record can help us understand past environments. I am particularly interested in Quaternary mammals from Korea and East Asia.

Quaternary mammals of Korea

Yonsei University Museum · March 2026–present

I collaborate with archaeologists at the Yonsei University Museum to identify Quaternary mammal fossils from Korean cave sites. This work involves revisiting existing collections and assessing their potential for further paleontological research.

These collections motivate my broader interest in mammalian paleoecology. I hope to investigate changes in faunal composition and distribution, and to relate those changes to environmental history in Korea and across East Asia.

Current focus: Fossil identification and development of research questions based on museum collections.

A Miocene talpid from Korea

Bukpyeong Formation, Donghae, South Korea

I am studying a small mammalian lower molar from the Miocene Bukpyeong Formation. My work focuses on comparative dental morphology, its identification as a talpid, and its implications for the fossil record and paleobiogeography of East Asian moles.

This project formed the basis of my undergraduate thesis and conference presentations in 2025 and 2026.

Status: Manuscript in preparation.

Taphonomy of a Miocene softshell turtle

Hagjeon Formation, Pohang, South Korea

I contributed to a study of a fossil softshell turtle, Pelodiscus cf. P. gracilia, from the Middle Miocene Hagjeon Formation.

My contribution focused on sedimentological and taphonomic interpretation, including how a freshwater turtle could have been transported into and preserved within a marine setting. I also contributed to checking the comparative literature.

Status: Published in the Journal of the Geological Society of Korea in 2025.

Korean fossil heritage and database research

National research project · March 2026–present

I work as a research assistant on a national project concerning the long-term planning of fossil site excavation and utilization in Korea, led by the Paleontological Society of Korea.

My work includes compiling research literature to support the development of a national paleontological database.

Earlier analytical experience

Crystallography Lab, Yonsei University · March–May 2024

I investigated compositional variation in a mosasaur tooth using X-ray diffraction and X-ray fluorescence, with analytical work at the Pohang Accelerator Laboratory.

This project introduced me to the use of analytical techniques for investigating fossil materials and their preservation.
